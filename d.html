FolderPlex is a fast, lightweight Plex client built for LG webOS 4+ TVs.

  • Fast: cached libraries open instantly, posters are resized by the server, navigation is tuned for older TV hardware.
  • Better remote navigation: clearly highlighted focus, long-press OK (or the blue button) for quick actions, focus returns to where you left off.
  • Your way of browsing: Plex metadata (including your manual match fixes) or the real folder structure from the server.
  • Sorting: by title, date added, release date or recently watched — remembered per library.
  • Play in order or shuffle any folder, library or TV show across all seasons, with auto-play of the next video and an adjustable countdown.
  • Playback: direct play with hardware decoding (MKV, MP4, HEVC, AC3), automatic fallback to server transcoding, resume and watch-state sync with Plex.
  • Interface languages: English and Russian.

Unofficial app, not affiliated with Plex Inc.
